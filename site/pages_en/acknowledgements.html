Word data

Vikey switches between English and Vietnamese using word statistics (how common each word is, and which words usually follow which) counted from these sources. The app contains only the statistics, never the text.

  • FrequencyWords (OpenSubtitles 2018), Hermit Dave (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  • FineWeb-2 and FineWeb, Hugging Face (ODC-By 1.0)
  • HPLT v2, High Performance Language Technologies (CC0)
  • MADLAD-400, Google (ODC-By 1.0)
  • Reddit comments, tensorshield/reddit_dataset_157 (MIT)
  • Vietnamese forums and reader comments, VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ, Tinhte, Otofun, Dạy Nhau Học (word statistics)
