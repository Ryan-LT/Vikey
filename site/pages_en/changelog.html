Changelog

1.0.14 · 29/10/2026

  • Smarter auto-switch for words that are both English and Vietnamese: Vikey also looks at the word before ("cho con bú" but "đi xe bus", "ở bên" but "it's been").
  • Built from over 180 million words of Vietnamese and English text: fewer wrong words and fewer manual switches.
  • "ạ" right after an English word no longer becomes "aj".
  • Vikey for Windows: first release, same typing engine and auto-switch as the Mac app (x64 and ARM64).

1.0.13 · 29/10/2026

  • More accurate auto-switch for chat-style Vietnamese: learned from 2.5 million words of forum posts and comments (ạ, đc, đt…).
  • "visa" and "meme" no longer become "vía" and "mêm".
  • "deepseek" no longer loses a letter ("deepsek").
  • "web(" no longer becomes "ưeb(".
  • All-caps words mid-sentence like NAS always stay as typed.

1.0.12 · 28/10/2026

  • All-caps words like NAS, ASUS, GoPro no longer get Vietnamese accents.
  • Typing Vietnamese with Caps Lock (TÔI ĐI HỌC) no longer gets stuck in English mode.
  • "Wi-Fi" no longer becomes "Ưi-Fi".

1.0.11 · 28/10/2026

  • Fewer manual switches: "cos", "nos", "sex" typed in English mode become "có", "nó", "sẽ" while you write Vietnamese.
  • "video call thí" becomes "video call this".

1.0.10 · 28/10/2026

  • The auto-switch pause after a manual switch is now 5 seconds.

1.0.9 · 28/10/2026

  • After you switch English/Vietnamese yourself, Vikey does not auto-switch for 15 seconds. You can turn this off in the Vikey window.

1.0.8 · 28/10/2026

  • Words after ( or " are fixed too: "(thí create" becomes "(this create".
  • Pressing Delete after Vikey fixed several words at once deletes one character instead of undoing.

1.0.7 · 28/10/2026

  • Pressing a tone key twice works like a familiar Telex keyboard: v e r r → "ver".
  • English words with double letters still come out right when you finish them: terrible, sorry, issue.

1.0.6 · 28/10/2026

  • The debug menu moved into the Vikey app menu, out of the menu bar icon.

1.0.5 · 28/10/2026

  • Typing "mĩ" then x to fix it into "mix" no longer gives "mixx".
  • Fixed tomorrow, surprise, express, seemed, cheese typed in Vietnamese mode.
  • Fixed a lost letter when typing "were" in Vietnamese mode.

1.0.4 · 28/10/2026

  • Automatic updates: Vikey checks for a new version every day and installs it.
  • Added "Kiểm tra cập nhật…" (Check for Updates) to the menu.

1.0.3 · 28/10/2026

  • English words before a quote or apostrophe are fixed too: "trang web", doesn't, isn't.
  • Remembering the language per website is off by default. When you turn it on, Vikey asks for permission to read the page address right away.
  • Added (?) buttons that explain each option.

1.0.2 · 28/10/2026

  • Recognises English sentences sooner: typing "thí makes" is fixed right away to "this makes".
  • Fixed the first word after switching back to Vietnamese sometimes not being checked.

1.0.1 · 28/10/2026

  • A tidier Vikey window.
  • Author and license information moved to the About Vikey window.

1.0.0 · 28/10/2026

The first version.

  • Auto-switch English/Vietnamese as you type: in English mode "treen" gives "trên", in Vietnamese mode "more" still gives "more".
  • Fixes the earlier word too once the sentence is clearly English: "thí makes sense" becomes "this makes sense".
  • Leaves programming words and variable names alone: var, const, git push, obj.dir, my_var.
  • Press Delete right after Vikey fixes a word to undo it.
  • Remembers the language for each app and each website.
  • Works with Dvorak and Colemak keyboards. Steps aside when you switch to another input method such as Japanese or Chinese.
