Privacy

What you type never leaves your Mac. Vikey only goes online to check for updates.

What you type

Vikey reads the keys you press to turn them into Vietnamese text, right on your Mac. Your keystrokes never leave it.

Website addresses

To remember the language for each website, Vikey reads the domain of the open tab in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. The first time, macOS asks for Automation permission. Firefox is not supported yet. Domains are stored only on your Mac.

Update checks

Once a day, or when you choose "Kiểm tra cập nhật…" (Check for Updates), Vikey downloads the list of versions from vikey.app. The request only includes the Vikey and macOS version numbers, nothing about what you type. Turn off automatic updates and Vikey only checks when you ask.

This website

vikey.app uses no cookies and does not track visitors.
